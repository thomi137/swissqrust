Expand description

Draws the four Eckmarken (corner marks) for an empty placeholder box.

Each of the four polylines in polylines represents one L-shaped mark, anchored to one corner of viewbox. Marks are re-anchored to the matching corner of rect at their fixed native size (not stretched to fill rect), since a payment-part box (e.g. 65x25mm) and its receipt counterpart (52x20mm) must show identically-sized corner marks.
